## 1. Network charge discounts for large flat loads: does a data centre qualify?

**Asked by the owner on 2026-11-07** (OPEN-QUESTIONS 216, question A2: "Add it to a further research list").

**Why:** the owner chose an optional tick for these discounts, off by default. The tick needs, for each country, a value and the conditions. Only Germany's rule has been researched.

**What is known (from `grid-capacity-charges-2026-11.md`):**

- **Germany:** bandload discount, StromNEV § 19(2). A customer with at least 7,000 use hours and more than 10 GWh a year can agree an individual charge of no less than 20 % of the published charge (15 % at 7,500 h, 10 % at 8,000 h), with the regulator's approval. A law firm blog (2026) says the ordinance runs to 31 December 2028, an extension to 2031 applies to existing customers only, and the future of the rule is to be decided in early 2027. Uncertain for a new campus.
- **Spain:** the electro-intensive status (Royal Decree 1106/2020) can reduce the charges. Whether a data centre qualifies was not checked.
- **Portugal:** electro-intensive customers get reductions on the policy-cost part (CIEG). Whether a data centre qualifies was not checked.
- **France:** French law has a network tariff reduction for electro-intensive sites. Not researched; use by data centres not checked.
- **Hungary:** the transmission energy charge is listed with a 100 % discount under MEKH decree 10/2024. Who qualifies was not checked.

**What is needed, per country:** whether a data centre qualifies (yes, no, only if conditions are met); the rule and its legal reference; the size of the reduction and what it applies to (network charge, levies, or both); the conditions (hours, volume, sector code, approval); the expiry or review date; the local website that states it (as for the data centre tax rules, OPEN-QUESTIONS 204). Also the same question for reductions on levies (for example Germany's Energiefinanzierungsgesetz reductions, Poland's 15 % and 25 % rates, Switzerland's refunds, Croatia's reduced classes, Slovenia's reduced contribution), which `levies-and-taxes-2026-11.md` records as "data centre taken as not eligible".

**Until then:** the tick uses Germany's researched rule; for other countries it stays unavailable or blank and flagged "no published value found".
