# New and planned rules for data centres in Europe (research of 11 November 2026)

Asked by the owner on 2026-11-11: "look up country new rules for DC that just came in or are planned for the future". Web research of that day. Every row names its source. Status words: **in force**, **adopted** (decided, not yet applying), **draft** (published, not decided), **announced**.

This note covers rules that bear on the power strategy of a campus: grid connection, on-site generation, consents, renewable and efficiency duties. Taxes and grid charges are in the November research notes in this folder and are not repeated.

The legal points are a reading of published texts and law-firm summaries, not legal advice. Each needs checking for a real project.

## 10. What the tool does with this

Decided by Claude under the owner's carte blanche of 2026-11-11 (see OPEN-QUESTIONS 247 onward); the owner can reverse any of it.

1. **Country rules become buildability tests.** A rule in force that an option breaks makes it "unlikely" (Ireland without dispatchable plant, an islanded gas campus in Ireland, a hyperscale campus in the Netherlands outside the two named areas). A rule that needs a decision by someone else, or a draft rule, becomes a named condition.
2. **The consent step in the programme** depends on plant size and country (300 MW thermal; England's 50 MW; Italy's ten months).
3. **Lead-time defaults** are replaced by the ranges in section 9, labelled "sourced — not independently checked".
4. **Not built from this note:** fault ride-through duties (a design matter), efficiency classes (the tool takes the PUE as entered, and flags Germany's 1.2 limit), tax changes (already in the November notes).

## 11. Limits

- One day of web research. Trackers and law-firm notes were used where the legal text was not read.
- Not covered: Greece, the Baltic states, Czechia, Hungary, Romania, Luxembourg, Iceland.
- Lead times are mostly United States figures.
- How long an environmental assessment takes was not researched. The programme uses a judgement.
