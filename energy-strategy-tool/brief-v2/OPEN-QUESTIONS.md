# Open questions

> **Standing instruction (owner, 2026-10-23, in writing):** "Continue the build with all the missing packages. I'm happy to follow your recommendation every time." From then on, open questions are answered with Claude's recommendation, recorded here as "Claude's recommendation, accepted in advance by the owner (2026-10-23)", each with its reason, so the owner can review and reverse any of them. Hard-to-reverse steps (merging into `main`, deleting branches, changing repository settings) still wait for the owner.

Two lists. Questions 1–18 come from the brief (revisions 1–4): each has a proposed default that the brief already uses, so building can start without an answer, and the last column says what the owner's walkthrough of 2026-10-21 did to it. Questions 19–35 are points the walkthrough left open or did not cover. They have **no proposed default**: the owner decides them one at a time. Ask the owner before building the part a question affects, and record the answer, with its date, here and in `BRIEF.md`. Related assumptions are listed by ID (see `ASSUMPTIONS.md`).
