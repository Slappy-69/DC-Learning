CFE counts PPA volume up to the residual load regardless of import capacity (dispatch.js:372): the T3 leader's CFE share is 0.7 % with the PPA off and 47.9 % with it on, with zero PPA energy delivered.
- **O3. Flag wording and severity.** The T1 leader's grid-ppa headline reads "PPA limited by import capacity", but only 140 MWh of the 4,475,368 MWh undelivered is import-limited; 4,475,228 MWh is above the load in the hour. Severity is "minor" for all 1,113 PPA options because any import-limited MWh triggers it (evaluate.js:464). The owner may want the headline to follow the larger part.
